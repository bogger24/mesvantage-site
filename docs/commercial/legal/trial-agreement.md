## 1. Definitions

1.1 "**AI Assistant**" means the optional AI-assisted drafting and query features of the Platform, which operate on a read-and-draft basis only, cannot approve, release, or sign any record, and do not use Customer Data to train any model.

1.2 "**Confidential Information**" means all information disclosed by one party to the other, whether before or after the Start Date, that is marked confidential or would reasonably be regarded as confidential, including (in the case of MESvantage) the Platform, its documentation, and pricing, and (in the case of the Customer) any Customer-Loaded Data.

1.3 "**Customer-Loaded Data**" means any data, other than the Seeded Demo Data, that the Customer uploads to or enters into the Trial Tenant, subject always to clause 4.2.

1.4 "**Intellectual Property Rights**" means all patents, rights to inventions, copyright and related rights, trade marks, trade names, rights in designs, database rights, rights in confidential information (including know-how), and all other intellectual property rights, in each case whether registered or unregistered, anywhere in the world.

1.5 "**Pilot Fee**" means the fee of €6,000 (six thousand euro), exclusive of VAT, payable in accordance with clause 3.

1.6 "**Platform**" means the MESvantage manufacturing execution system software, including modules for device history records (DHR), genealogy and traceability, OEE, FAI/inspections, NCR/CAPA, scheduling, audit trail, electronic signatures, the AI Assistant, and OEM customer portals, together with all updates and documentation.

1.7 "**Seeded Demo Data**" means the fictitious demonstration dataset pre-loaded into the Trial Tenant by MESvantage.

1.8 "**Start Date**" means the date on which MESvantage provisions the Trial Tenant and sends the Customer access credentials.

1.9 "**Trial Period**" means the period of thirty (30) days beginning on the Start Date.

1.10 "**Trial Tenant**" means the hosted, isolated evaluation environment of the Platform provisioned by MESvantage for the Customer.

2.2 The Customer receives no rights to the Platform other than those expressly granted. No licence to the Platform's source code is granted.

2.3 MESvantage may suspend or revoke access to the Trial Tenant at any time if the Customer breaches this Agreement or if MESvantage reasonably believes continued access poses a security, legal, or reputational risk. Where practicable, MESvantage will notify the Customer before suspension.

2.4 MESvantage may update the Platform in the Trial Tenant at any time. Features available in the Trial Tenant may differ from those in a production subscription.

## 3. Term, Payment and Conversion

3.1 **Payment method capture.** At signup, the Customer must provide a valid payment method, which is captured and stored by MESvantage's payment processor, Stripe. MESvantage does not itself store full card details. By providing a payment method, the Customer authorises MESvantage to charge the Pilot Fee as described in this clause 3.

3.2 **No charge during the Trial Period.** No amount is charged to the Customer's payment method during the Trial Period.

3.3 **Charge at day 30.** Unless the Customer cancels before the end of the Trial Period under clause 3.4, MESvantage will charge the Pilot Fee of €6,000 (plus VAT, if applicable) to the Customer's payment method on the day following the end of the Trial Period.

3.4 **Cancellation.** The Customer may cancel at any time before the end of the Trial Period via the cancellation facility in the Trial Tenant or by emailing hello@mesvantage.com. If the Customer cancels before the end of the Trial Period: (a) nothing is charged; and (b) the Trial Tenant, including any Customer-Loaded Data, will be deleted in accordance with clause 7.4.

3.5 **Credit on conversion.** If the Customer enters into a Master Subscription Agreement (or equivalent subscription contract) with MESvantage for an annual plan within [90 days] of the end of the Trial Period, the Pilot Fee will be credited in full against the one-time implementation fee payable under that agreement.

3.6 **Expiry of access.** Unless the parties agree otherwise in writing, access to the Trial Tenant ends at the end of the Trial Period regardless of whether the Pilot Fee is charged.

3.7 **No automatic subscription.** Nothing in this Agreement obliges the Customer to purchase a subscription, and no subscription begins automatically at the end of the Trial Period.

## 4. Acceptable Use

4.1 **Evaluation only.** The Trial Tenant is a sandbox evaluation environment. The Customer must not use the Trial Tenant or the Platform during the Trial Period:

   (a) for production use of any kind, including any use in the manufacture, inspection, release, or distribution of any medical device, component, or any other product;

   (b) as part of, or in reliance on, any validated process; or

   (c) to create, store, or process any genuine device history record, batch record, quality record, or other production data.

4.2 **No real data.** The Customer must not upload to or enter into the Trial Tenant:

   (a) any real device, batch, serial number, or production data;

   (b) any patient data, health data, or other special categories of personal data;

   (c) any personal data other than the names and business contact details of the Customer's own users; or

   (d) any data belonging to any third party without that party's permission.

   If the Customer breaches this clause 4.2, MESvantage may delete the affected data and/or the Trial Tenant, and the Customer indemnifies MESvantage in accordance with clause 10.4.

4.3 **Technical restrictions.** The Customer must not, and must not permit any person to:

   (a) copy, modify, translate, or create derivative works of the Platform;

   (b) reverse engineer, decompile, or disassemble the Platform, except to the extent this restriction is prohibited by applicable law;

   (c) attempt to gain unauthorised access to any part of the Platform, the Trial Tenant, or any other customer's environment;

   (d) probe, scan, or test the vulnerability of the Platform without MESvantage's prior written consent; or

   (e) use the Platform to build a competing product or service, or permit access by any competitor of MESvantage.

4.4 **User conduct.** The Customer is responsible for the acts and omissions of its users and for keeping access credentials confidential.

7.3 **Analytics.** The MESvantage marketing website uses Plausible, a cookieless, EU-hosted analytics service, and sets no cookies. Use of the Platform itself may generate operational logs (for example, access and audit logs) that MESvantage processes to operate and secure the service.

7.4 **Return and deletion.** At the end of the Trial Period (or on earlier cancellation), MESvantage will, at the Customer's written request made within [14 days], make Customer-Loaded Data available for export in a reasonable machine-readable format. Following that period, the Trial Tenant and all Customer-Loaded Data will be deleted within [30 days], except to the extent retention is required by law.

7.5 **No production data processing agreement.** Because no production or special-category data is permitted in the Trial Tenant (clause 4.2), no data processing agreement applies to the Trial Period. Any production subscription will be subject to MESvantage's Data Processing Agreement.
